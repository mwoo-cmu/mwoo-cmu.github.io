Enemies & AI Behavior

  • Enemy Primary and Secondary Units have an j deck. On the enemy turn, draw an Order card from their Order deck. Play cards from the selected enemy's j deck equal to their j number.
  • The Enemy Primary Unit also has a i deck. Whenever it is Wounded, play a card from their i deck.
  • Whenever an enemy Unit makes an attack, remove one die from the attack roll for each Wound or Injury it has.

Grid & Movement Mechanics

g Distance

Count the number of orthogonal spaces in the shortest path from one object to another.

p Shove

Move affected character 1 g away (backwards/diagonally backwards). The shoving character may then move 1 g toward them.

Pull

Move affected character towards pulling character (forwards/diagonally forwards) or along shortest path to them, even if Euclidean distance increases.

x Advance / Dash

Move up to 5 spaces (through allies, not enemies); must end on empty space. If Engaged, convert to h instead.

h Reposition

Move up to 2 spaces through both allies and enemies; must end on an empty space.

t Jump / Fly

Move to any other empty space within Line of Sight (LOS).

Line of Sight (LOS) & Cover

Two grid spaces have LOS if an uninterrupted line can be drawn from any point within one grid space to any point within the other. Cover condition is met if an interrupted line exists between any point in attacker's space to defender's space.

Locations & Tokens

  • Event Triggers: Each time you reshuffle your Order deck, draw an Event card and resolve its effects immediately.
  • Mission Token: A marker used for tracking scenario objectives. Specific interactions are defined by the current Mission.
  • Priority Objective Token: A marker used primarily by the Enemy AI deck to determine targeting and movement vectors.

Stacking Supporting Units

If the board is getting cluttered or miniature availability is limited, you may choose to stack Supporting Units together.

  • The Unit Stack has the combined Stamina (r) and Durability (w) of all stacked Supporting Units.
  • The stack's dice pool for attack and defense increases by +1 die for each extra character added to it.
  • Each time the Unit Stack is Injured, remove one character from the stack.
